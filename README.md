NOVADRIFT — a gravity-orbit arcade game. Pilot a spark-ship around a dying star: hold to thrust outward, release and gravity pulls you in. Harvest light-shards on three rings (the inner ring pays most, but the heat burns), dodge asteroids, outrun telegraphed solar flares, and chain pickups for combos up to ×8. Three hull hearts — make them last.

## Run it

```sh
npx serve game
```

Then open http://localhost:3000 in a browser. Controls: hold SPACE or CLICK to thrust outward, A / D to steer along the orbit, P to pause, M to mute.
